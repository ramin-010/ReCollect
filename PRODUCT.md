# PRODUCT.md — Recollect

_Written 2026-11-14 by the design lead (Claude) from 2–3 months of Ramin's real use of his own app "Revise". Change it when reality says otherwise._

## What it is
Recollect is where a working software engineer keeps what he is learning alive, and where the things he needs again live: revisions due today, his goals, and the notes and commands he reaches for every day.

**Direction (Ramin, 2026-11-14):** a buddy that keeps him organised (learning, goals, tasks, decisions, schedule), in sync with the coding agents he spends his day in, on PC and phone; first for him, later for students, office learners and coders. His chats and editor do the work; Recollect holds the plan and the record, and agents keep it up to date. Nothing may ever be a required log. The plan: `context/specs/BUDDY_PLAN.md`.

## Who uses it
**Ramin, first and for now only.** He's a software engineer at a small company in India, earning about ₹30k a month, and wants to grow fast while AI reshapes the field.
- He learns mostly DSA, plus backend, system design and AI.
- He uses Claude Code all day at work.
- Making it a SaaS comes later, and only if it truly works for him.

## His moment
- **The start of the day, on a laptop** (1366–1920 wide). It's the browser's start page or the first tab, opened before or at the start of work. He has a couple of minutes, wants to see what matters today, and then gets on with it.
- **During the day, a quick return** to copy a pinned command (e.g. the ssh/update-server command) or jot something down. Instinctive, a few seconds.
- **Sometimes on the phone** (390 wide), to tick off a revision or read a note. _(Assumed. Ramin to confirm.)_

## What he learned the hard way (the design must respect this)
- **Rigid time blocks plus "Action Required" logs made him stop opening the app.** Logging every missed block felt like failure, then guilt, then avoidance.
- **A busy month at work buried him in overdue items** (one sat 35 days). The app must make coming back cheap.
- **Only two things gave daily value:** today's due list, and pinned quick notes.
- **"A goal without a deadline is just a fantasy."** The deadline goes on the goal; the flexibility goes on the day.

## Principles
1. **Opening the app never costs anything.** No pop-ups, no required logs, nothing to "catch up on" before he can see his day.
2. **Today is short and finite.** The list fits a daily budget (default about 25 min). Overdue items are not a red wall: they wait quietly and come in as room allows. One tap gives a fresh start.
3. **Coming back is the most important flow.** After days away: "Welcome back. Just these 5 to get going."
4. **Measure learning, not hours.** Consistency is shown as a gentle weekly rhythm, not a fragile streak or a percentage of blocks.
5. **Goals have dates; days are flexible.** The app forecasts honestly ("on track" / "about 3 weeks behind") and suggests the smallest adjustment. It never scolds.
6. **The pocket is a tool.** Pinned notes and commands are one click to copy and one keystroke to find.

## Screens and their jobs (one line each)
- **Home / Today:** see today's few revisions and focus lines, do them, and leave. Glance at goal progress and the pinned pocket.
- **Revision item:** recall the topic and mark it done (one click); open its doc when you need to re-read.
- **Pocket (Quick notes):** find and copy a saved command, snippet or link in seconds; jot a new one.
- **Goals:** set 1–2 goals with a deadline and a few milestones; see the honest forecast.
- **Capture (Ctrl+K):** put anything in (task, note, "revise this") without leaving the current screen.
- **Settings (learning):** daily budget, busy mode, morning nudge, timezone, and routine (off by default).

## Tone
Calm, plain, encouraging without cheerleading. Sentence case, active verbs, short. Never guilt ("You missed 4 days!"); always the next small step ("5 things today, about 20 min").
